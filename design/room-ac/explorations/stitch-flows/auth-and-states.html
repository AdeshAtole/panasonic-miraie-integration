  • Sign out: Clears the locally stored token and returns to Screen 1. Does not delete Panasonic credentials or unpair physical hardware.
  • Widget Pinning: Prompts the Android OS AppWidgetManager.requestPinAppWidget system sheet without bespoke launcher permissions.
verified Operational Limits & Architecture Checklist

• Modes: Exactly Auto, Cool, Dry, Fan (strictly no Heat).
• Temperature Range: 16–30°C in 1°C steps with draft/commit boundary.
• Fan Speeds: Auto, Quiet, Low, Medium, High; Auto only in Dry.
• Features: Eco (sets 26°C in Cool only); Powerful (Cool only, mutually exclusive with fixed capacity).
• No Unsubstantiated Telemetry: No live RPM, no humidity, no background battery drain, no automatic mutation without readback verification.
