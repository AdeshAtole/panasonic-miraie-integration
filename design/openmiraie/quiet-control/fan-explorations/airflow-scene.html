Aim the air. One scene, two dimensions.

Grab the stream.

With both swings off, drag in any direction. If one axis is swinging, drag along the other axis—the active swing keeps moving. Release to snap only the fixed axes to supported positions.

Snap guides appear only along the axes you can adjust. Arrow keys also work from the horizontal bar.

Each axis has its own swing.

Turn on Vertical, Horizontal, or both. Vertical swing still allows left/right aiming; Horizontal swing still allows up/down aiming. Manual aiming is disabled only when both swing. Turning one off returns that axis to its last confirmed fixed position. It does not freeze the AC at the animation’s instantaneous angle.

Direction, not telemetry.

The photo is an AI-generated representative AC. Flow movement illustrates reported settings; it is not a camera feed, physical airflow measurement or a verified vane angle.

The proposed horizontal order is 3–2–1–4–5. The mapping needs physical verification before production use.

Honest when only one axis changes.

While dragging, the stream previews your aim locally. After release, a dashed outline shows the requested direction and the blue stream follows reported state. Try “Horizontal fails” to see a partial update. The axes are not assumed to update atomically.

Converti8 Off means Normal automatic capacity. Its fill marks a supported preset, not measured power. Fixed capacity and Powerful replace one another with confirmation. Design prototype only. No appliance connection or Android changes.
