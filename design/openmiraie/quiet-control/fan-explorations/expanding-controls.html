Open. Adjust. Carry on.

Try the photo + draggable airflow concept →

Point the air. Tap a line. No slider, no extra step.

One calm surface.

Tap either card to open its choices. Only one card expands at a time, keeping the other within reach.

Auto, or your speed.

Tap Auto, or slide between Quiet, Low, Medium and High. Four distinct stops, with only the chosen speed named above the track.

Release to apply. The card stays open for another adjustment; tap its header to close.

Two axes. Independent control.

Tap a line or its endpoint to select that airflow position immediately. Vertical runs from position 1 at the top to 5 at the bottom. Horizontal places 1 in the centre, 2–3 to the left, and 4–5 to the right. Oscillate remains a separate button.

The small value under each axis is its last reported setting.

Honest feedback.

The selected line stays on the reported position. A dashed line marks a request awaiting readback. Matching readback moves the selection; an unconfirmed request leaves it unchanged.

The drawing uses your proposed layout: left to right, 3–2–1–4–5. Its physical mapping is not verified and must be checked before connecting these positions to an AC. The curves illustrate direction, not measured angles. These implementation notes stay outside the customer screen.

Design prototype only. Sample data; no appliance connection. Native app implementation awaits your approval.
