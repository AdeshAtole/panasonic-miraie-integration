Create the primary controller screen as a native mobile app, helping a person change comfort settings and turn the AC's illuminated display off at night. Use the project's existing design system. **PLATFORM:** Mobile, Android-first. Design a single portrait controller at a typical 390dp content width, respecting system status/navigation insets. Content can scroll when needed; the primary controls should fit within the first typical phone viewport. Touch targets are at least48dp.
